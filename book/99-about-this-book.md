# About this book


## Who wrote it

**Bartłomiej Krupa** builds and ships software with coding agents. He writes about the part nobody puts in the demo: what fills the context window, what the bill responds to, and why a session that looked fine produced work that was not. He publishes at **bartlomiejkrupa.dev** — the Agentic Engineering Terminal. This book is the reference he wanted on his own desk.

## Colophon

Written as hand-set HTML and rendered to PDF by a headless browser — no word processor and no layout application in the chain. The contents and the index are built by measuring every section of the real document, which is why their page numbers are exact rather than close. Mono ligatures are off throughout: a font that joins -- into one stroke makes a command-line flag unreadable at the moment someone is retyping it.

- **READING TYPE** — Source Serif 4, 11.2/16.8pt, ~71 characters
- **HEADINGS** — Inter, 800–900 weight
- **TECHNICAL TYPE** — Geist Mono, ligatures off
- **PAGE** — A4, 146mm text column
- **PAPER AND INK** — #F5F1E8 · #1C1A17 · #BC0100
